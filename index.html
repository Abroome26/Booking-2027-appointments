2027 Appointment Booking
Admin training · Splose

Booking 2027 ongoing appointments

Book each participant's 2027 appointments as one repeating series ending 25 December 2027, then email them their list. Do one participant at a time.

Watch the walkthrough videoOpens in Loom

How to do it

Nine steps. Tick each one off in the checklist as you go.

  1. 1
    Service Agreement board

    Find the participant

    Open the Maya view, go to Until booked 2026 and sort by Service. Pick a participant of your clinician (for example, Joss = Psychology).

  2. 2
    Splose

    Check their pattern

    Open the participant's Appointments tab. Note their next appointment and how often they come.

  3. 3
    Splose

    Check the case

    Check the participant's Case.

  4. 4
    Splose · Details

    Turn off reminders

    Go to Details > Edit. Record their current reminder setting in the box at the top of this page, change it to None, and save.

  5. 5
    Splose · Calendar

    Book the series

    1. Click their next 2026 appointment > Book another > the slot at the same time > Appointment.
    2. Untick Case and check the date and time.
    3. Add the booking note (copy it below).
    4. Repeat on their usual cycle until 25 Dec 2027, then click Create.
    Booking note
  6. 6
    Splose

    Clean up

    1. The conflicts shown are their existing 2026 bookings. Click Continue to create.
    2. Check each new appointment. Cancel any that clash with the clinician's availability.
    3. Cancel public and school holidays only if the participant's details ask for it.
    4. Archive the old series.
    5. Add the new appointments to the service case.
  7. 7
    Splose · Details

    Turn reminders back on

    Set the reminder setting back to what it was, and save.

    Don't skip this. Otherwise the participant gets no reminders for any 2027 appointment.
  8. 8
    Email

    Email the participant

    1. Download the upcoming appointments PDF and rename it.
    2. Use the email template, add the contact and CC the clinician.
    3. Fill in the last 2026, first 2027 and last 2027 dates.
    4. Add Kate's unavailability blurb and remove the BSP blurb.
    5. Attach the PDF, check it over, send.
    PDF file name
  9. 9
    Service Agreement board

    Update the board

    Mark the participant as done and add a note.
